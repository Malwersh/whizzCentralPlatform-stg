Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and follow the instructions. You'll receive a verification code via email to reset your password.
How do I add or edit products?
Go to Products → Add Product or tap on an existing product to edit. You can add photos, set prices, manage inventory, and more.
How do I manage incoming orders?
All new orders appear in the Orders tab. You'll receive push notifications for new orders. Tap on an order to view details, accept, or reject it.
How do I change my business location?
Go to Settings → Business Profile → Location. Tap on the map to set your precise business location. Make sure location services are enabled on your device.
How do I set my working hours?
Navigate to Settings → Working Hours. You can set different hours for each day of the week and mark days as closed.
Why am I not receiving order notifications?
Check: 1) Notification permissions are enabled in Settings → Notifications, 2) Your internet connection is stable, 3) The app is up to date. If issues persist, contact support.
How do I update my payment information?
Go to Settings → Payment Settings to add or update your bank account information for receiving payments.
Can I temporarily close my store?
Yes! Use the "Store Status" toggle in the app to temporarily close your store. Customers won't be able to place orders while closed, but your profile remains visible.
How do I delete my account?
Contact support at write2ghayth@outlook.com with your account details. We'll process your request within 5-7 business days. Note that this action is permanent.
