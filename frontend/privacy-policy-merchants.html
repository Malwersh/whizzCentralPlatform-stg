Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Whizz Merchants ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). This App is designed for merchants to manage their business operations, including orders, products, and customer interactions.

By using the Whizz Merchants App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our App:

2.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: Email address, phone number, business name, and password when you create an account.
  • Business Information: Business address, business type (restaurant, cafe, retail), operating hours, and payment information.
  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, and email address for communication purposes.

2.2 Location Data

  • Business Location: We collect precise location data to help you set your business location on the map for customer visibility and delivery purposes.
  • Usage: This data is used only when you explicitly set or update your business location.
  • Permission: You can control location permissions through your device settings.

2.3 Camera and Photo Library Access

  • Product Photos: We request access to your camera and photo library to allow you to upload product images for your business listings.
  • Document Uploads: Camera access may be used for uploading business documents (licenses, certifications).
  • Usage: Images are only accessed when you choose to upload them.

2.4 Order and Transaction Data

  • Order details (items, quantities, prices)
  • Customer information (delivery addresses, contact details)
  • Payment processing information
  • Transaction history and sales records

2.5 Device and Usage Information

  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • App usage statistics (features used, time spent)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, error logs)
  • Push notification tokens for order alerts

2.6 Communications Data

  • Messages exchanged with customers through the App
  • Support requests and correspondence with our team
  • Notifications and alerts preferences

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

3.1 To Provide and Maintain Our Service

  • Process and manage orders received through the platform
  • Display your business location to customers
  • Enable product catalog management
  • Facilitate payment processing
  • Provide real-time order notifications

3.2 To Improve Our Service

  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Develop new features and functionality
  • Monitor and analyze trends
  • Perform technical diagnostics and troubleshooting

3.3 To Communicate With You

  • Send order notifications and alerts
  • Provide customer support
  • Send administrative information (policy updates, security alerts)
  • Deliver marketing communications (with your consent)

3.4 For Security and Legal Compliance

  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and investigate complaints

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

4.1 With Customers

  • Business name, location, and contact information are visible to customers
  • Product listings and prices are publicly displayed
  • Operating hours and availability status

4.2 With Service Providers

  • AWS (Amazon Web Services): Cloud hosting and data storage
  • Payment Processors: To process merchant payouts and transactions
  • Notification Services: To deliver push notifications
  • Analytics Providers: To understand app usage (anonymized data)

4.3 For Legal Reasons

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To respond to lawful requests from authorities
  • To protect our rights and property
  • To investigate fraud or security issues

4.4 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Authentication: AWS Cognito for secure user authentication
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls and authorization mechanisms
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and updates
  • Secure Infrastructure: Hosted on AWS with enterprise-grade security

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Active Accounts: We retain data while your account is active
  • Transaction Records: Financial records retained for 7 years (legal requirement)
  • Deleted Accounts: Personal data is deleted within 90 days of account deletion (except where legal retention is required)
  • Logs and Analytics: Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for analytics

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

7.1 Access and Portability

  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Export your business data in a portable format

7.2 Correction and Update

  • Update your account information through the App
  • Correct inaccurate information

7.3 Deletion

  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Note: Some data may be retained for legal compliance

7.4 Location Permissions

  • Enable or disable location access through device settings
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Whizz Merchants

7.5 Camera and Photo Access

  • Enable or disable camera/photo access through device settings
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera/Photos > Whizz Merchants

7.6 Push Notifications

  • Enable or disable notifications in App settings or device settings
  • iOS: Settings > Notifications > Whizz Merchants

7.7 Marketing Communications

  • Opt-out of promotional emails by clicking "unsubscribe"
  • Manage notification preferences in the App
