# Dated store delivery agreements and optional driver tips

The accepted delivery campaign remains unchanged. A separate version-two commercial contract defines a free or fixed customer delivery fee, a gross-goods minimum, named regions, a start/end time, the payer or proportional split of the actual tariff shortfall, and the platform commission percentage. The commission can decrease or increase. These contracts have no campaign budget, per-order subsidy ceiling or customer-use counter.

Staff sends the exact version through the existing authenticated delivery workflow; the owner or authorized manager accepts its hash. Publication stores immutable history and a public projection containing only dates, regions, customer fee and minimum. Overlapping agreements for a region/period fail closed. There is no synthetic consent or migration of the existing accepted campaign.

The authoritative source verifies the live commercial parties, exact terms and lifecycle guards. An active agreement takes precedence over promotional campaigns in its region, including when browsing an empty cart. The order writer checks the agreed minimum, calculates the agreed commission, and freezes the exact funding/commission amounts into the confirmed financial snapshot. Ending the period restores the original merchant minimum, underlying tariff and original commission for new orders. Existing orders retain their confirmed terms.

Campaign allowance and cash funding are separate. Each agreement order still reserves actual platform cash when needed and creates a conditional immutable funding reservation. Capture, cancellation, return, reassignment and acceptance use the original order reservation and money holds. No unlimited wallet balance is fabricated. A fixed fee may exceed the underlying tariff; in that case its subsidy is zero, never negative. Shared shortfalls round the merchant portion down to whole IQD and assign the remainder to the platform.

The customer checkout now offers no tip, suggested IQD tips and a custom whole-IQD amount. The default is zero. The selected amount is included once in the request, summary and server consent review. Tip controls are disabled for pending confirmation. The existing financial allocator keeps the tip separate from delivery support, commission and the goods minimum.

Public business responses carry the original minimum and approved agreement projection, allowing the shared client to show fixed terms before routing finishes. Valid previews are reused when reopening the store; goods pricing and route calculation run concurrently. Address scope and agreement start/end boundaries invalidate cached results. Fresh confirmation remains authoritative.

Staging publication changes twelve captured Lambda packages through bounded overlays. It preserves handlers, IAM, configuration and all unrelated ZIP entries. Shared financial functions reuse each host's existing AWS SDK command constructors; a second SDK is not loaded into customer Lambdas. Equivalent aliases on live condition checks are deduplicated without dropping differing predicates or splitting OR predicates.

Validation: 1,201 server tests; 51 tests against the packaged shared runtime; six actual order-handler tests including commission, overriding minimum, fixed delivery, tip and atomic confirmation; 13 customer Flutter tests; 11 merchant Flutter tests; four public-facts tests; Chrome mobile tests for both campaign and agreement forms. Customer and merchant profile builds were installed on iMohammed. The new commercial agreement has not been sent or accepted by the operator; physical order/settlement verification remains pending.
