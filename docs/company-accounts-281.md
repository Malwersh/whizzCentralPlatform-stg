# Company accounts workspace (281)

New company workspace replaces the obsolete financial-management redirect and legacy rules UI. Wallet funding/merchant settlement operations remain at finance.html, separately labelled. Reports are generated server-side from posted immutable source journals, not storefront prices or a page's loaded rows.

Sources: full cash journals in MerchantPlatform; independent cash adjudication and exception entitlement journals in Holds; posted cashbox ledgers (deduplicated by journal ID, including transfers); reconciled merchant payout journals; completed historical COD allocations (explicitly labelled legacy net contribution); company accounting documents in PlatformStaff. Outcome rows are order statistics/evidence, not additional GL entries when a canonical journal exists. Unposted approvals never count as expense. Driver-unsettled refunds reduce custody and are separately disclosed, not fabricated company expense.

Period bounds are inclusive Iraq dates (UTC+03), converted to a half-open UTC interval. Money is integer IQD minor units with scale 1000. Journal debit=credit and unknown account/suspect source warnings are explicit. Failed/incomplete scans fail reports; no partial page sum is presented as a total. Current wallets/debts are labelled current observations, not historical period-end balances; GL opening/closing are from supported journals. Source scan consistency is per-item, not an atomic cross-table financial close. Saved statements freeze the exact report and source digest without locking ongoing operational writers.

Company overhead documents: immutable proposals, independent current finance approver, amount limits and staff version guards, proof-reference deduplication, explicit accrual or external-account accounting. No writes to operational cashbox balances or driver/merchant wallets. Existing cashbox spending is entered through its existing custody workflow and imported, not entered twice. External company-account entries represent documentary postings, not initiation of a bank transfer. Opening-equity and receivable/payable documents can represent documented legacy positions. Reversals append opposite postings and preserve original documents; no deletion or editing of posted journals.

- Browser flows at 390 px and 1440 px verify RTL layout without page overflow, proposed overhead documents remaining unposted, immutable saved statements, period-wide CSV, and complete A4 PDF ledger output.
- The exact report route read current staging sources and matched ORD1792679331051 to net -450 IQD, company expense 750 IQD, and actual declared cash 8,500 IQD. October 1–22 source inventory produced 10 completed financial outcomes, 2 returns, 1 cancellation, 15 unique GL journals and no source issues at verification time.
- A temporary fixed read-only Lambda using the real staff execution role successfully read all projected tables and order references, then was deleted. No financial document, account balance, customer statement or saved company statement was created on staging by the agent.
- A saved statement is immutable evidence of the supported sources at its read time. It is not a statutory financial close. Cross-table reads are not an atomic snapshot. Current operational positions are labelled current, not historical end-of-period cash.
- Reading is bounded to 200 pages / 25,000 rows per source and the service request deadline. Exceeding a bound fails the report, never returns a partial total. Saved statements are capped at 2.5 MB. Large-history installations need a scheduled journal index/report job before increasing these limits.
- Counterparty subledger postings reject payments/collections exceeding documented balances, enforce per-account version fences and one opening per account or debt party, and reject duplicate proof/reversal claims. Receivable/payable opening balances do not invent profit or loss.
- Historical data outside canonical supported journals and genuine external company account balances must be established by documented opening entries. Driver/merchant/customer wallet balances are not company revenue or a substitute for bank reconciliation. No existing financial history was backfilled.
